# Candidate FAQ Sheet

## DC Operations Junior Associate

---

# Role Purpose and Responsibilities

**1. What is the main purpose of this role?**
The main purpose of this role is to support the day-to-day operations of our distribution center so that our customers receive the right material, on time, and in good condition. The position focuses on picking, staging, replenishment, maintaining inventory accuracy, and keeping the facility organized and safe. It is a hands-on warehouse role and does not involve driving or delivery duties.

**2. What would a typical day look like?**
A typical day involves picking and staging orders using handheld barcode scanners, replenishing inventory locations, assisting with receiving and transfers, performing cycle counts, maintaining a clean workspace, and coordinating with supervisors and team members to meet operational priorities.

**3. Will I be interacting with customers?**
Not often. This role has limited to no direct customer interaction. Most interaction is with distribution center staff and sales staff.

**4. Will I be driving a company vehicle or making deliveries?**
No. Driving and delivery duties are not part of this role.

**5. What types of equipment will I use?**
You may use handheld barcode scanners, forklifts, pallet jacks, and other material handling equipment. Forklift use requires certification, which the company provides.

---

# Training and Experience

**6. Do I need warehouse experience to be hired?**
A high school diploma or GED is required. Prior warehouse or material handling experience of at least six months is strongly preferred but not required.

**7. Will I receive training?**
Yes. Training is a major part of this role. You will be trained across the core functions of the distribution center, including receiving, putaway, picking, auditing, packing, staging, loading, cycle counting, replenishment, product recognition and identification, safety procedures, general maintenance, and housekeeping standards. You will also be trained on handheld scanning systems and warehouse processes. The goal is to make sure you understand not just how to do the tasks, but why they matter to the overall operation.

**8. How long does training usually take?**
Training is most intensive during the first one to two weeks. During that period, you can expect structured, hands-on instruction and close supervision. After that, training continues intentionally over the next two to three months as you build confidence and expand into additional responsibilities. Beyond that point, development continues as part of normal work, but the bulk of formal onboarding will have already taken place.

**9. Will I be cross-trained in multiple warehouse functions?**
Yes, with some defined limits. Cross-training across most distribution center functions is an essential part of the role. However, this position does not initially include cross-training in driving, delivery, shipping, receiving, or wire cutting. Those areas can become opportunities later as part of growth and promotion. Being cross-trained in the core warehouse functions is expected and helps prepare you for advancement opportunities.

---

# Work Environment and Physical Requirements

**10. What are the physical requirements of the job?**
This role requires prolonged standing, walking, bending, and reaching throughout the shift. You will frequently lift up to 50 pounds and occasionally up to 100 pounds with assistance. Although the work is indoors, the warehouse is not climate controlled in the same way as an office. It can be hot in the summer and cold in the winter. Some tasks involve limited outdoor work, such as handling conduit in the yard, cycle counting in outdoor storage areas, or assisting with basic facility maintenance like clearing, salting, or sweeping walkways.

**11. Is the work performed indoors or outdoors?**
Most of the work is performed indoors in the warehouse. However, the building is impacted by seasonal temperatures, so conditions vary throughout the year. There is also occasional outdoor work related to yard inventory and light facility maintenance responsibilities.

**12. Will I be required to work overtime?**
Overtime is not constant, but it can be required. Most often, if needed, it would occur at the end of a shift when we need to ensure trucks are loaded and ready for nightly replenishment. That decision is typically made late in the day based on workload. In addition, all distribution center staff are expected to participate in annual physical inventory, which takes place in the spring. This may require one or two weekend days per year. Outside of those situations, overtime is occasional and based on operational demand.

**13. What safety measures are in place?**
Safety is a core expectation in the distribution center. Employees follow established safety procedures, use appropriate protective equipment when required, and maintain an organized, hazard-free workspace. We emphasize communication of potential safety concerns and consistent use of standard operating procedures to ensure material is handled properly and tasks are completed safely.

# Schedule and Work Arrangement

**14. Is this an onsite position?**
Yes. This role is fully onsite at the distribution center.

**15. What are the typical work hours and shifts?**
There are several shift options within the department. Typical schedules include:

* 7:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
* 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
* Four-day shifts Monday through Thursday
* 8:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. Monday through Thursday

Exact assignment depends on operational needs.

**16. Are shifts fixed or rotating?**
Shifts are not contractually guaranteed; however, once assigned, they typically settle into a consistent schedule. We aim to create stability in scheduling whenever possible.

**17. Are weekend shifts required?**
Weekend work is very rare. The primary exception is the annual physical inventory in the spring, which requires participation from distribution center staff. Outside of that, weekend work would only occur for special projects and is often handled on a volunteer basis.

# Performance Expectations and Evaluation

**18. How is performance measured?**
Performance is measured by how accurately and efficiently you complete your work, how well you maintain inventory accuracy, how consistently you follow safe work practices, and how you contribute to keeping the workspace organized. In simple terms, we look at quality, pace, safety, and reliability.

**19. What does success look like in this role?**
Success begins with taking care of the customer by ensuring orders are accurate and ready when needed. It also means working efficiently, maintaining inventory accuracy, following safety procedures, keeping the facility organized, and contributing positively to the team.

**20. How often is performance reviewed?**
Performance feedback is ongoing and informal. Supervisors monitor day-to-day accuracy, productivity, attendance, and safety. Coaching happens in real time so expectations stay clear and issues are addressed early. We do not currently conduct formal annual review cycles for this role.

---

# Supervision and Team Structure

**21. Who will I report to?**
You will report to the distribution center manager.

**22. Will I be supervising other employees?**
No. This role does not have supervisory responsibilities.

**23. How large is the team I will be working with?**
When fully staffed, the distribution center team consists of approximately 11 to 14 employees.

---

# Equipment Certification and Advancement

**24. Will I be required to operate forklifts?**
Forklift operation may be required after completing company certification training.

**25. Does the company provide forklift certification?**
Yes. Forklift training and certification are required before operating equipment, and the company provides both the training and certification process.

**26. Are there opportunities for advancement from this role?**
Yes. This role is an excellent entry point into the company. We encourage employees to learn the operations around them and take initiative in developing their skills. Advancement opportunities may include expanded distribution center responsibilities, leadership roles, or movement into other departments such as sales or operations.

**27. Can employees move into other roles within the company over time?**
Yes. Experience in the distribution center builds strong operational knowledge and product familiarity, which can support movement into delivery, sales, operations, or other areas of the company.

# Compensation and Benefits

**28. What is the hourly pay range for this position?**
The starting rate for this position is $14.00 per hour, with scheduled increases to $14.50 at six months and $15.00 at twelve months, contingent upon satisfactory performance, attendance, and policy compliance.

**29. Are overtime hours paid at a higher rate?**
Yes. Any hours worked over 40 in a workweek are paid at time and a half.

**30. What benefits are offered?**
We offer a comprehensive benefits package including:

* Medical insurance (Highmark BCBS PPO and HSA-eligible plans)
* Dental coverage (Blue Edge Dental Flex 3W)
* Vision coverage (VSP Choice Network)
* 401(k) retirement plan with employer matching
* Life insurance and disability coverage
* Paid time off and company holidays
* Employee Assistance Program
* Employee purchase discount program

**31. Are there attendance bonuses or performance incentives?**
No. Compensation is based on the hourly wage structure rather than separate bonuses.

# Workplace Policies and Expectations

**32. What type of dress code or uniform is required?**
Employees must maintain a neat, clean, and professional appearance appropriate for warehouse work. Acceptable attire includes work pants, jeans, appropriate shorts, t-shirts, collared shirts, and sweatshirts. Clothing must be safe, appropriate, and free from offensive graphics. Proper hygiene and grooming are expected.

**33. What personal protective equipment (PPE) is required?**
Appropriate protective equipment is required depending on the task, including safety footwear, gloves, or other gear as needed.

**34. What is the company’s approach to safety?**
Safety is treated as a daily operational priority. Employees are expected to follow procedures, communicate hazards, and maintain organized work areas.

# Hiring and Onboarding Process

**35. What does the hiring process involve?**
The hiring process includes application review, interviews, possible skills assessments, background check, drug screening, and formal offer and onboarding.

**36. Is there a probationary or introductory period?**
Yes. New employees are hired on a ninety calendar-day trial period to ensure mutual fit.

**37. How soon could I start if hired?**
The full hiring process typically takes approximately one month from application to start date, depending on scheduling and pre-employment requirements.
